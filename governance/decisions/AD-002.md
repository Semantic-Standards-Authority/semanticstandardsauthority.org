AD‑002 — Authority Decision: Approval of RA‑1 (Regulatory Antinomy Semantic Class)
Version: 1.0
Status: Final
Authority: Semantic Standards Authority (SSA)
Document Class: Authority Decision
Identifier: AD‑002

1. Decision
The Semantic Standards Authority hereby approves Standard S2 — Regulatory Antinomy Semantic Class (RA‑1) as an official semantic standard of the Authority.

2. Basis
This decision is issued under the powers defined in:

SSA Charter

Authority Declaration

SSA‑GOV‑0001 (Governance Framework)

The Authority confirms that RA‑1 meets the required criteria for:

semantic clarity,

structural consistency,

interoperability alignment,

deterministic operational behaviour,

academic independence.

3. Scope of Approval
This decision approves:

the full text of RA‑1 v1.1,

its semantic unit structure,

its operational behaviour rules,

its lifecycle classification as a semantic standard,

its institutional independence clause.
