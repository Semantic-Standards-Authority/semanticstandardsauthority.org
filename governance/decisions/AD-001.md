# AD‑001 — Authority Decision: Approval of S1 (Semantic Unit Specification)
Version: 1.0  
Status: Final  
Authority: Semantic Standards Authority (SSA)  
Document Class: Authority Decision  
Identifier: AD‑001  

## 1. Decision
The Semantic Standards Authority hereby approves **Standard S1 — Semantic Unit Specification** as an official SSA semantic standard.

## 2. Basis
This decision is issued under the SSA Charter, SSA‑GOV‑0001, and the Authority’s mandate to define and maintain semantic interoperability structures.

## 3. Scope of Approval
This decision approves:
- S1 v1.0  
- its semantic unit structure  
- its role as the foundational specification for all SSA semantic artefacts
